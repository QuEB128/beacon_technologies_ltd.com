STEWARD V.A.

See Beyond. Move Ahead. Live Freely.

A wearable AI assistant designed to help people who are blind or have low vision better understand their surroundings and perform selected everyday tasks.

STEWARD V.A. product concept — compact, tactile and intentionally simple.
Product Capabilities

What STEWARD does today.

01

Object Recognition

Helps identify common objects within the user's environment.

02

Text Reading

Uses OCR technology to extract and read text from documents, signs, labels and other visual materials.

03

Currency Recognition

Designed to recognize supported Ghanaian currency notes and provide audio feedback.

04

Scene Description

Provides useful descriptions of elements within the user's surroundings.

05

Obstacle Detection

Uses sensor technology to provide real-time environmental feedback about nearby obstacles.

06

Navigation Support

Provides audio guidance intended to support safer movement.

Connectivity

When connectivity isn't guaranteed.

Selected STEWARD functions are designed to operate locally or offline, reducing dependence on continuous internet connectivity.

Design Philosophy

Designed around people, not assumptions.

STEWARD has evolved through direct interaction with visually impaired users. Their feedback has influenced the physical design, interaction model, camera positioning and system performance.

Current Status

Pilot Development & Commercialization Preparation

STEWARD V.A. is currently undergoing pilot development and manufacturing preparation. Product features and performance continue to evolve through testing and user feedback.

Technology

Intelligence designed for the real world.

The components behind STEWARD.

Computer Vision

Used for object recognition, scene understanding and visual interpretation.

OCR

Used to extract and read text from visual documents and environments.

Machine Learning

Recognition models are trained and evaluated for specific tasks.

Sensor Technology

Provides real-time environmental feedback for obstacle awareness.

Edge / Local Processing

Selected capabilities are designed to operate locally without continuous cloud connectivity.

Audio Interaction

Visual and system information is converted into understandable audio feedback.

Data & Localization

Local data matters.

During development, the team identified areas where suitable African datasets were limited.

For currency recognition, Ghanaian cedi notes were collected and used to train and evaluate the currency recognition models.

For object recognition, a combination of downloaded datasets and models trained by the team has been used for common objects.

Progress

Built. Tested. Improved.

STEWARD V.A. is being developed through an iterative process of building, testing and learning from real users.

User Validation

We started by listening.

A structured user validation exercise was conducted with six visually impaired participants.

The validation helped identify areas requiring improvement, including physical comfort, prototype size, button interaction, camera positioning, response latency, AI performance, audio feedback and general usability.
